Ron Assistant

    Ask once. Hear from every model.

    Type a question below. In Review together, the models you turn on answer separately, check one another’s answers, and one writes a final answer. In Compare, you see each answer side by side.

    Answers are printed here first. Only the final answer is spoken, unless you ask for another.

    Ctrl+Enter to ask

    Settings

    My saved instructions and preferences

    Sent with every question to each model you turn on. Kept separately from conversation history and never deleted by clearing history.

    Models and connections

    Uses the sign-ins already saved on this computer. A ChatGPT or Claude subscription does not include API access, so this app does not use API keys.

    Review together

    Conversation memory

    Earlier questions and final answers from the same conversation help the models follow up. They share a fixed length, so sending more makes each one shorter rather than the question longer. + New conversation always starts fresh.

    Voice

    Speak final answers with

    The Jarvis avatar reads answers through your existing Jarvis app. Its access key stays in Jarvis’s own folder and is never sent to a model. Jarvis accepting the text does not prove sound reached your speakers. The avatar uses your Jarvis Cloud avatar minutes.

    Conversation history

    When off, conversations are forgotten when you close the app, and nothing new is saved. Your saved instructions are not affected.

    Updates

    The installed app asks GitHub (github.com) whether a newer Ron Assistant has been released, at start-up and every six hours, and downloads it in the background. Nothing about your questions, answers or settings is sent. The new version is installed when you close the app, or straight away if you choose Restart now.
